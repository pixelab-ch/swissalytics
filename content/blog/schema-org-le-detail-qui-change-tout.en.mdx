## Why it matters now

AI engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini — read raw HTML, not your JavaScript. JSON-LD is the only format they can reliably use to understand **who** you are.

> "Without JSON-LD, your site looks like a stranger claiming to be someone. With it, it looks like an identifiable entity."

## The three blocks to deploy first

1. **Organization.** Who you are. Legal name, logo, address, contact, social profiles. This is your machine-readable ID card.

1. **LocalBusiness (where relevant).** Opening hours, coordinates, service area. Mandatory if you have a physical location or a defined geographic zone.

1. **FAQPage.** The questions your clients actually ask, paired with your answers. LLMs love pulling structured question-and-answer pairs.
