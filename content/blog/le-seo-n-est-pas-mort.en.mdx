On lit partout que **le SEO est mort**. C'est faux, mais c'est une demi-vérité utile. Ce qui est mort, c'est le SEO comme optimisation de mots-clés. Ce qui commence, c'est quelque chose d'un peu plus exigeant.

## The real shift

In 2024, Google introduced AI Overviews. In 2025, ChatGPT surpassed 400 million weekly users. The common thread: people no longer click ten blue links — they read a synthesised answer that *cites* its sources.

## What concretely changes

1. **Intent has replaced the keyword.** You no longer target an exact query — you target a *need*. LLMs paraphrase, synonymise, and broaden.

1. **Citations have replaced clicks.** Being cited in an AI answer, even without a click, is now worth more than a cold 30-second visit.

1. **Structure has replaced volume.** A well-structured 500-word article beats a poorly organised 3,000-word one. LLMs extract by section.
