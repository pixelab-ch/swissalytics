In June 2025, **Terre des hommes** reached out to us: they rarely appeared in ChatGPT answers about child aid in Switzerland. By October, they were cited in **42 %** more relevant queries.

## Starting point

The site was technically clean but **invisible** to an LLM: no JSON-LD Organization, no FAQPage, named entities rarely present in page body text. Starting Swissalytics score: 58/100.

## The eight fixes applied

1. **Complete JSON-LD Organization.** With logo, founding date, mission, area of action, and contact. Deployed within 48 hours.

1. **FAQPage on key pages.** Seven recurring donor questions, structured in JSON-LD and mirrored as visible text on the page.

1. **Rewritten `h1`s and meta descriptions.** With named entities (name, founding year, countries of action) instead of slogans.

> "Eight fixes, zero redesign. The site looks identical to a human visitor — it is unrecognisable to an LLM."

## Measured outcome

Tracked across 80 test queries (child protection, aid, Switzerland, Lausanne). Before: cited in 19 answers. After: cited in 27. **+42 %**. Swissalytics score: 58 → 84.
